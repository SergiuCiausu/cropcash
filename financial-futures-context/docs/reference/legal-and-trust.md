# Legal and trust rules

Get a Romanian fintech lawyer's review before moving real money or launching investing. Phase 1 avoids most of this by
simulating every regulated part.

| Area | Rule for the app |
| --- | --- |
| Bank data | Get customer consent, use licensed access, and never ask for banking passwords |
| AISP route | Account information providers register, with lighter requirements and insurance or a comparable guarantee |
| PISP route | Payment initiation providers need payment institution authorization; minimum capital listed as €50,000 |
| User money | Avoid holding funds unless a lawyer confirms the setup |
| Payments | Require approval, and expect strong authentication under PSD2 |
| Investing | Let users choose rules; personalized stock recommendations can be regulated advice |
| Personal data | Meet GDPR requirements for financial and family data |
| Affiliate links | Label commissions clearly and rank by value, not commission |
| Website data | Prefer official feeds, and check terms before scraping |
| Automation | Show history, allow undo where possible, and keep users in control |
| Food safety | Treat allergen data as guidance and tell users to check labels |

**Automation levels:** Categories, shopping lists, and alerts can run automatically. Goal changes and suggested
contributions need one tap. Payments, orders, and stock purchases need clear approval or a user-created rule they can pause.

**Regulatory watch:** PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation were agreed but not in force as of June 2026, with
application estimated around 2028. Check their current status before finalizing compliance.

**Licensing for the hackathon:** none needed. A PSD2 license from the National Bank of Romania takes months; production
access goes through a licensed partner (Salt Edge Partner program) instead.
