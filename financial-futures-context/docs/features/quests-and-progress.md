# Quests and progress

Users pick a starting plan that sets their first quests, and they can switch plans at any time without losing progress.
Everything appears in the normal app interface as lists, checkmarks, and progress bars, with no characters, battles,
or game artwork.

| Starting plan | First quest | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safety first | Build a 3-month emergency fund | Slower lifestyle spending |
| Travel | Fund the next trip | Long-term goals may take longer |
| Home and family | Save for a deposit | Less short-term flexibility |
| Early retirement | Raise the monthly savings rate | Less spending today |
| Balanced | Split savings across goals | Each goal moves more slowly |

Ana picks **Travel** in the demo.

## How quests work

- **Quests:** Small real actions, such as scanning a receipt, canceling an unused subscription, or making a contribution.
- **Challenges:** A bigger problem split into steps, such as clearing forgotten subscriptions, paying off a debt, or recovering from a surprise car repair.
- **Guided order:** Budgeting, goals, investing, and retirement open in sequence as earlier steps are done.
- **Future impact:** Each completed quest shows how the projected future changes.
- **Decision comparison:** Compare major decisions side by side.
- **Household quests:** Shared quests for family members.

**Rules:** The starting plan never sets investment risk. Reward healthy habits, not trading or bigger risks.
Don't punish setbacks, and don't rank people's money on leaderboards.

## Progress points

Points measure real progress the app helped the user make, not time spent in the app.

| Point source | When it counts | Safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money saved | A saving is confirmed, such as a canceled subscription that stays canceled | Estimated or one-off deal savings don't count |
| New income | The user confirms it came from an app suggestion, and it appears in their account | Regular raises and unrelated income don't count automatically |
| Quests | A real action is completed | Each quest pays points once |
| Good habits | The user completes a weekly review or reaches a goal contribution | Limit repeat points so they can't be farmed |

- **Fairness:** Score savings and income relative to the user's own income, not just total lei.
- **Real progress only:** Transfers between the user's own accounts earn nothing, and a canceled saving removes its points.
- **No risk rewards:** Loans, investment gains, and spending never earn points.
- **Protected progress:** Milestones don't reset after a hard month, and they stay private.
- **Proof:** Tapping a point total shows what earned it, such as "Canceled unused streaming plan: saves 40 lei a month".
