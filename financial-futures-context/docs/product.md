# Product

The app is a money copilot that helps people spend less, earn more, and see where they're heading.
Its promise: **Know your money, keep more of it, and grow your future.**

It responds to the "Rethinking Financial Futures" brief by making saving, investing, and retirement more accessible,
flexible, and personal. First market: Romania. Early users mainly have RON accounts.

## Five pillars

| Pillar | What the app does | How to make it realistic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear view | Brings income, expenses, goals, and net worth into one place | Connect banks, scan receipts, and let users correct anything |
| Save everywhere | Finds savings in bills, subscriptions, shopping, and daily purchases | Start with the biggest recurring costs, then expand by category and store |
| Read the user's mind | Predicts what the user is likely to want and what could happen next | Learn from behavior, ask quick questions, and show why it made a suggestion |
| Earn more | Suggests realistic ways to increase income | Match ideas to the user's skills, time, location, and risk tolerance |
| Plan the future | Projects net worth, goals, and retirement paths | Show likely ranges, then update them after every major change |

Goal deposits and rule-based investing turn these insights into action. The home screen stays simple by showing what
the user has, what they can do today, and whether they're on track.

## Customer journey (Ana)

Ana's turning point is booking her vacation: it proves the plan works and earns trust for longer-term decisions.

| Stage | Ana's situation | What the app does | Ana thinks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Money disappears | No vacation and no plan | Connects her accounts and shows one clear view | "Where does my money go?" |
| 2. Sees the real problem | Small leaks add up | Finds bills, subscriptions, and spending patterns | "I can fix this." |
| 3. Gets a quick win | Frees up monthly money | Suggests canceling, switching, or shopping cheaper | "This was easy." |
| 4. Books the vacation | Reaches her first goal | Automates monthly saving and celebrates progress | "I actually did it." |
| 5. Starts investing | Uses small amounts and her own rules | Runs approved investing rules | "I'm building something." |
| 6. Feels secure | Has a safety cushion and a clear future | Updates retirement and net worth forecasts | "I know where I'm heading." |

**Setback moment:** If Ana has an unexpected car repair, she can pause a contribution and change the goal date.
The app treats this as using the plan, not failing it.

**Demo story:** The €40 a month Ana was wasting becomes her vacation, then the start of her future.
In the mock data it is exactly 198.98 RON (FitZone 129.00 + StreamPlus 39.99 + PhotoEdit Pro 29.99) at 4.97 RON/EUR.

## Demo script

Ana connects her bank, finds a subscription, chooses the Travel plan, saves toward Greece, handles a pause, asks about
a car loan without syncing it, compares retirement paths, then upgrades to Premium through Stripe Checkout.

**Done when:** a judge can follow Ana's full journey in a few taps without a live scraper, bank, broker, or store integration.

## Automation levels

Categories, shopping lists, and alerts can run automatically. Goal changes and suggested contributions need one tap.
Payments, orders, and stock purchases need clear approval or a user-created rule they can pause.
